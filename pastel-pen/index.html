Firefox releases · Equation reading fixture

Read, mark, revisit

Pen by the page

Turn the pen on beside an article. On a narrow page, the switch moves to the lower corner. The browser toolbar still offers path and domain rules.

Highlight the whole passage

Drag through prose and equations in either direction, then choose a colour and save. Highlights follow the browser’s text selection and leave the article’s markup in place.

Notes in context

Attach a sticky note, drag it into place or collapse it to a pin. Notes support Markdown and TeX math. Each notebook opens a continuous reading copy, with highlights and notes alongside the original text.
